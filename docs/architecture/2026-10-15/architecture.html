Forecast, publication and outcome pipelines

Detailed control flow: freeze evidence before results; distinguish internal capture from observed public availability.

Forecast and publication

1Fetch as-of sources
2Generate base → serve once
3Journal before dedupe
4Gate exact generation
5Publish + verify hashes
6Export + durable ACK

Failure path before publication

  • Unknown status → quarantine its identifiable row.
  • Stale source or hash mismatch → reject with reason.
  • Keep R102 checks and R106 archive protection.

Failure path after publication

  • Engine down → preserve outbox; retry same IDs.
  • Conflicting payload → reject; never overwrite.
  • ACK only after commit; alert before kickoff.

Outcome and learning

1Authoritative FINAL
2Append revision chain
3Pin exact cohort/revisions
4Chronological evaluation
5Shadow candidate
6NFL gate + owner review
Three clocks: source availability → forecast creation → internal/database observation. A later import cannot prove earlier capture. Public availability needs a separate deployment/content-hash observation.

Sampling boundary

Pool rungs, MY offers and Game/Week cards are distinct populations. Compare the same declared cohort; copies are not independent samples.

Scheduling and recovery

Actions completion + manual dispatch; cron is best effort. Durable leases/checkpoints recover repeated or killed jobs.

Learning and adoption states

Statistical and serving authority stay separate. Uploading evidence or fitting a model is not permission to use it.

Equation contract

Generic Platt: sigmoid(a × confidence + b). NFL: sigmoid(a + b × logit(immutable base)), then clamp and round. These are not interchangeable.

Timing and rollback

Current MY policy needs ≥100 legs and ≥2 held-out week folds: normally ≥3 receipt weeks. R127 separately needs ≥2 new graded weeks. Rollback changes future serving, not history.

Do not refit to Week 5 or one TNF result. Candidate population, base model, artifact hash, evaluation revisions and effective version must all remain traceable.
